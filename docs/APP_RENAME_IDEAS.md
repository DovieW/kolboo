# App Rename Ideas

A brainstorming list of potential new names for the app (currently: **Tambourine Voice**).

## Notes

- **“Shtima”** is **Yiddish for “voice”**.
- Some options below have strong religious/mythic associations; that can be either a feature (memorable, bold) or a drawback (polarizing, context-heavy).

---

## 1) Mythic / “spoke to God” energy (e.g., _Moses_)

Names that feel like _a message received_ or _a messenger delivering a voice_.

- **Moses** — strong, simple, memorable; explicitly religious-coded.
- **Sinai** — evocative and mystical; less direct than “Moses.”
- **Oracle** — classic “divine voice” vibe; may collide with other products/brands.
- **Prophet** — bold and clear; slightly intense.
- **Revelation** / **Revel** — “a message revealed”; works well for transcription/insight.
- **Herald** — messenger/announcer vibe; strong “app name” feel.

## 2) Latin / Greek roots for “voice”

### Latin

Latin “voice” is **vox**; the _voc-_ root relates to calling/voice.

- **Vox** — extremely short; very “voice”; potentially common.
- **Voxa** / **Voxo** — modern, brandable variants.
- **Voxis** — distinctive; “sounds Latin.”
- **Vocalis** — literal/clear; a bit longer.
- **Vocare** — Latin for “to call”; classy.
- **Vocem** — a Latin form of _vox_; looks ancient/techy.

### Greek

Greek “voice” is often rendered as **phōnē** (root of “phone”).

- **Phona** — short and brandable.
- **Phonea** — a little more mythical/soft.
- **Phonē** — accurate vibe; diacritics may be inconvenient in product naming.

## 3) Clean, modern “software name” style

Names that fit a desktop/Tauri tool and don’t require explanation.

- **Tambourine** — keeps existing brand DNA, but simpler than “Tambourine Voice.”
- **Pulse** / **Pulsevoice** — waveform/audio feel.
- **SaySo** — compact and friendly.
- **SpeakEasy** — fun pun (also a known term).
- **WhisperKey** — good if hotkeys are a core feature.
- **HotMic** — punchy; “oops hot mic” meme connotation.

## 4) Unique / coined names (distinctive like _Shtima_)

If the goal is “memorable first, explained later.”

- **Shtima** — uncommon and brandable; meaning ties directly to voice.
- Variants: **Shtyma**, **Shtema**, **Shtimo**
- Similar “made-up but pronounceable” energy:
  - **Voshka**
  - **Vezma**
  - **Tymra**
  - **Shivra**
  - **Kivra**

## 5) Names tied to what the app does (voice → text + overlay + history)

Names that hint at transcription, logging, recall, or continuous capture.

- **EchoLog** — “voice in, history out.”
- **SpeakLog**
- **AudioTrail**
- **Recall**
- **ClipNote**
- **LiveScribe**
- **VoiceVault**

---

## Shortlist (balanced: memorable + not-too-generic)

- **Sinai**
- **Herald**
- **Voxa**
- **Vocare**
- **EchoLog**
- **Shtima**

## Quick narrowing prompts

1. Should the name feel **serious/pro**, **playful**, or **mystical**?
2. What’s the primary identity: **transcription**, **voice commands**, or **overlay/streaming companion**?

(Answering those makes it much easier to pick a final top 5 and write taglines.)
